# FoodBridge — Smart Surplus Food Redistribution Platform

> **Bridge surplus food to people who need it.**

FoodBridge is a web-based platform designed to reduce food waste by connecting **food donors** such as hotels, restaurants, caterers, cafeterias, bakeries and event venues with **verified NGOs and receivers** who can collect and redistribute surplus food.

## 🌱 What is FoodBridge?

Large amounts of prepared food are often wasted after restaurants, hotels and events. At the same time, many organizations struggle to access sufficient food for the communities they support.

FoodBridge creates a digital bridge between **surplus and need**.

### 🔄 How it works

```text
Donor
  ↓
Post Surplus Food
  ↓
Nearby Receivers Discover It
  ↓
Receiver Accepts
  ↓
Pickup & OTP Verification
  ↓
Food Collected
  ↓
Impact Recorded
```

## 👥 User Roles

### Donors

Hotels, restaurants, catering companies, cafeterias, bakeries, event venues, hostels, food businesses and individuals can list their surplus food and manage donations.

### Receivers

Verified NGOs, shelters, community organizations and food-distribution groups can discover, accept and collect available food.

### Admin

Administrators can manage users, verify organizations, monitor donations, manage reports and view overall platform statistics.

## 🧠 Smart Matching

FoodBridge can prioritize donations based on:

* Distance
* Food urgency
* Quantity
* Pickup deadline
* Receiver availability

This helps make the food rescue process faster and more efficient.

## 📊 Impact

FoodBridge aims to track measurable impact such as:

* Meals rescued
* Food waste prevented
* Successful donations
* Successful pickups
* Active donors
* Verified receivers

## 🤖 AI Enhancement

AI can optionally be used for:

* Food categorization

AI is designed as an enhancement to the platform rather than its core purpose.
